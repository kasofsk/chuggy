## Next: PR 8c-2 — `UpdateTicket`

This needs David's design call before any brief is written. Survey §4 frames the question.

- **What must be settled.**
  - Today a `draft` holds authoring before release: the brief, links, checks, repository, configuration pin and `authoringVersion` fence. Nothing can edit a ticket after release; `revise_draft` answers `NotDraft`.
  - The package adds `UpdateTicket({ticket, expectedRevision, definition})` on a **Pending** ticket, plus `Ticket.revision`, `TicketUpdated`, and the three refusals 8c-1 declared but cannot reach: `TicketIdentityMismatch`, `TicketRevisionStale`, `TicketDependenciesChanged`.
  - The question is whether the draft stays the editor before release and `UpdateTicket` the editor after, which makes two editors of one thing, or whether release-then-update replaces draft revision.
  - Either way, the ticket page gains an edit offer, and `TicketProvenance.tsx` moves off the draft read.
- **Release coupling.**
  - `TicketUpdated` is a journal event, and `Ticket.revision` is journalled state.
  - The journal validator gains an arm, and the wire and console gain an offer.
  - Whether it needs a wipe depends on whether any existing event's text moves. Probably not: a new arm is additive, so it would be a migration with no wipe. Confirm this when writing the decisions.
- Estimate: smaller than 8c-1 in code, but carries design and console work.
