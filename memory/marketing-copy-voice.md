Marketing copy for chuggy (chug.gy) sells the vision of why it's being built and what teams need now. It must not be false, but it must not narrate implementation either (no actor/fabric/journal/phase names outside the developer section). Geoff 2026-09-19 called the first draft "tell-your-friends bad, hilariously claudeish, nonsense no business would ever put".

Positioning (Geoff 2026-09-19, after two rejected drafts that were "way too technical"): chuggy is "Squarespace for any app". You describe an app in plain words; infinite AI helpers shepherd every task to done; "not vibe coding but vibe management / vibe PMing". Audience includes people who don't know what a repo or merging is, without pushing away strong engineers (they get a For developers section). Say "AI helpers" early; define vibe management plainly. Hosting is part of the vision (Geoff 2026-09-19): chuggy hosts what the helpers build, with paved paths to common infrastructure (accounts, data, payments, messaging, files, domains) that the helpers already know; sell it as "approve and it goes live".

**Why:** internal vocabulary plus AI cadences (X-not-Y, colon reveals, stacked triads, cute closers, aphorisms) read as machine-written and sell nothing.

**How to apply:** write as consumer product marketing (Squarespace/Canva voice): describe it, AI helpers build and test it, you approve what goes live. Technical detail (GitHub, PRs, checks, the Quint-proved scheduler) lives only in the For developers section, and is precise there. Before showing copy, have a fresh copy-editor agent flag AI-isms and check every claim against the product (e.g. budgets can be refilled on resume, so they aren't "unoverridable"). The design spike canvas is https://claude.ai/artifact/79mmd5SeWTeL6wPt6WP1qp. Distinct from the in-app UI standard [[chuggy-ui-copy-standard]].
