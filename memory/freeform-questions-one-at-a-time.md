Geoff, 2026-09-08, after an AskUserQuestion menu offering three deploy paths:
"Please remember when you ask me questions like that to ask them free form
one at a time."

**Why:** a multi-option menu bundles several decisions and forces a pick from
the options as written; Geoff prefers a conversation where each question is
answered on its own terms.

**How to apply:** when a decision is the user's, write the question as plain
prose in the reply, one question per turn, with the recommendation stated. Do
not use the AskUserQuestion tool. See also the `push-back-and-verify-assumptions`
skill.
